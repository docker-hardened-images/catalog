## About Linkerd Tap

[Linkerd](https://linkerd.io) is a CNCF service mesh for Kubernetes. Tap is the viz extension component that streams
live requests: the `api` APIService and the `injector` webhook.

This image is the `tap` and `tap-injector` containers the Linkerd viz Helm chart runs.
