## About GoKubeDownscaler

GoKubeDownscaler is a Kubernetes controller that scales workloads down during a configured downtime window and brings
them back when that window ends. Schedules can be set for the whole cluster or overridden with annotations on a
namespace or a workload, which is how teams keep non-production environments from running around the clock.

Schedules and annotations are documented at https://kube-downscaler.io/ and
https://github.com/caas-team/gokubedownscaler.
