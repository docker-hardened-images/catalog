## About Snipe-IT

[Snipe-IT](https://snipeitapp.com) is an open-source web application for tracking IT assets, licenses, accessories, and
the people they are assigned to. The source is [grokability/snipe-it](https://github.com/grokability/snipe-it).

This image serves Snipe-IT with nginx and PHP-FPM on ports 80 and 443. Uploads, backups, and keys are stored under
`/var/lib/snipeit`, and MySQL or MariaDB runs as a separate service. Snipe-IT is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
