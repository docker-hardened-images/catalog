## About Kiali Operator

Kiali Operator watches Kiali custom resources and installs, updates, and removes Kiali instances on Kubernetes and
OpenShift. This image includes the upstream Kiali Ansible roles, playbooks, watch configurations, and pinned Ansible
collections.

For full documentation, see the [Kiali installation guide](https://kiali.io/docs/installation/installation-guide/).
